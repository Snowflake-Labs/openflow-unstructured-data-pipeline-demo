    Festival Operations: Document Intelligence Analytics¶

    Overview¶

    This document analyzes the Google Drive document collection from our Festival Operations demo to identify key analytics opportunities for Snowflake Intelligence and Cortex Search demonstrations using unstructured business documents.

    Data Foundation¶

    Document Collection Summary¶

    • Total Documents: 16 converted business documents across 4 formats (3 PDF, 2 PPTX, 2 DOCX, 9 JPG)
    • Business Categories: 4 operational areas (Strategic Planning, Operations Excellence, Compliance & Risk, Knowledge Management)
    • Document Types: 4 file formats (PDF, PPTX, DOCX, JPG) for multi-format intelligence processing
    • Time Span: January 2024 - September 2024
    • Business Scope: Festival operations, strategic planning, compliance, training

    Business Document Authors (Persona-Driven)¶

    The document collection includes 6 realistic business personas representing different organizational roles and document authorship styles:

    Executive Leadership¶

    • Sarah Thompson (CEO): Strategic visionary, confidential planning documents. Focus: Market expansion, competitive strategy
    • David Rodriguez (CFO): Financial analysis expert, data-driven decision maker. Focus: Budget planning, financial performance

    Operations Management¶

    • Mike Chen (Operations Manager): Process optimization specialist, high collaboration. Focus: Venue operations, project management
    • Emma Wilson (Safety & Compliance Manager): Regulatory expert, safety-first approach. Focus: Health policies, compliance standards

    Customer-Facing Teams¶

    • Jessica Taylor (Customer Experience Manager): Service excellence focus, staff development. Focus: Training materials, service standards
    • Ryan Martinez (Facilities Manager): Infrastructure specialist, vendor relationships. Focus: Contract management, facility operations

    Document Category Distribution¶

    The document collection spans 4 critical business areas optimized for multi-format intelligence demonstration:

    Category 1: Strategic Planning & Executive Intelligence (25%)¶

    • 2025 Festival Expansion Strategy (JPG Visual): Market analysis diagrams, revenue growth charts, geographic expansion maps
    • Board Meeting Minutes Q4 2024 (DOCX Collaborative): Executive decisions, strategic governance, quarterly reviews
    • Q3 2024 Financial Analysis (PDF Formal): Revenue performance, cost analysis, investment ROI calculations

    Category 2: Operations Excellence & Technology Modernization (25%)¶

    • Sound System Modernization Project Charter (DOCX Collaborative): $2.8M technology investment, 18-month timeline
    • Venue Setup Operations Manual (JPG Visual): Standardized procedures, safety protocols, equipment management diagrams
    • Post-Event Analysis Summer 2024 (PPTX Presentation): Performance review, incident analysis, improvement recommendations

    Category 3: Compliance & Risk Management (25%)¶

    • Health & Safety Policy (PDF Formal): Medical procedures, safety standards, regulatory compliance requirements
    • Audio Equipment Service Agreement (PDF Contract): Vendor contracts, service level agreements, legal terms

    Category 4: Knowledge Management & Staff Development (25%)¶

    • Customer Service Training Guide (PPTX Presentation): Staff onboarding slides, service excellence frameworks
    • Cross-referenced Operations & Meeting Documents: Procedural knowledge and leadership context integration
