## Advanced Demo Customization

### Combining Categories

- **Executive + Operations** (15 mins) - Strategic decisions backed by operational insights
- **Operations + Compliance** (12 mins) - Process improvement with risk management
- **All Categories** (20 mins) - Comprehensive organizational intelligence demonstration

### Custom Audiences

- **Board Meetings** - Focus on Strategic + high-level Operations insights
- **Department Heads** - Combine relevant categories for cross-functional leadership
- **Technical Teams** - Emphasize architecture, scalability, and integration across all categories

### Multi-Stakeholder Presentations

Each demo includes **transition suggestions** for moving between audience segments during larger presentations.

!!! success "Pro Tip: Demo Flow"
    Start with your primary audience's category, then use **"Now let me show you how this applies to..."** transitions to demonstrate cross-functional value without losing engagement.
