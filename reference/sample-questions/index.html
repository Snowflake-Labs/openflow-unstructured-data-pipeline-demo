    Demo Flow Suggestions¶

    Quick Demo (5-10 minutes)¶

    1. Start Simple: "What are our 2025 expansion plans?"
    2. Show Multi-format: "Find technology projects with budgets"
    3. Demonstrate Intelligence: "What health and safety policies exist?"

    Comprehensive Demo (15-20 minutes)¶

    1. Strategic: Market expansion and financial analysis
    2. Operational: Technology projects and venue management
    3. Compliance: Policies and vendor agreements
    4. Cross-category: Multi-format intelligence queries

    Executive Demo (30+ minutes)¶

    • Full business intelligence across all categories
    • Decision support scenarios with real business context
    • ROI demonstration showing time savings and insights

    Tips for Presenters¶

    Question Preparation¶

    • Bookmark this page for quick reference during demos
    • Practice questions with your specific data setup
    • Customize examples to match your audience's interests

    Demo Flow¶

    • Start simple with single-category questions
    • Build complexity with multi-format queries
    • End with impact showing business value

    Audience Targeting¶

    • Executives: Focus on strategic and financial questions
    • IT Teams: Emphasize technology and operational queries
    • Compliance: Highlight policy and risk management capabilities
    • General Business: Mix categories to show versatility
